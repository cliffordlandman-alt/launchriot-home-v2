What you get

One private workspace, set up for you. It is live today and in daily use in a paying customer’s workspace.

  • Email in one place

    Your inbox with the important messages at the top, so you see what matters first.

  • Your calendar

    Today’s meetings and who is attending, in the same place as your mail.

  • Chat messages

    Read your work chats alongside email, instead of switching between apps.

  • Tasks and staff to-dos

    Capture a task in seconds and see what your team owes you.

  • Follow-ups

    See the emails you sent that are still waiting for a reply.

  • Drafts you approve

    Replies are prepared as drafts. You read, edit and decide. Nothing goes out on its own.

How it works

  1. Book a demo

    A short call. We show you the workspace with sample content and listen to how you work.

  2. We set it up with you

    You choose what to connect. We do the setup together, step by step. It is a hands-on setup, not a self-serve app.

  3. You stay in charge

    Open your workspace, check what needs you, and approve the drafts you want to send.

You stay in control

  • Nothing is sent, deleted or changed without your approval.
  • You choose what to connect, and can disconnect at any time.
  • Your workspace is kept separate from every other customer.
  • You can export your data or ask us to delete it.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Directors and business owners who live in their inbox, calendar and chats, and want one place to see what needs them.

No. It prepares drafts. You approve each one before anything is sent.
