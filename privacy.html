Privacy notice

This explains, in plain words, what the LaunchRiot workspace handles and why.

1. What we access

Only what you choose to connect, with your permission: your email, your calendar and, if you choose, your chat messages. Connections are read-only. We do not access anything you have not connected.

2. How we use it

To show you your own mail, calendar and messages in one place, to put the important items first, to track tasks and follow-ups, and to prepare draft replies. Nothing is sent, deleted or changed without your approval.

3. No outside AI services

Your messages are not sent to outside AI services. Sorting and highlighting use fixed rules inside your workspace.

4. We do not sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not use it for advertising. People at LaunchRiot do not read your messages unless you ask for support and give permission, or the law requires it.

5. Each workspace is separate

Every customer has their own separate workspace and database. Your data is not visible to other customers.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data only while your workspace is active, plus short-term backups. You can disconnect an account at any time, export your data, or ask us to delete it, and we will confirm when it is done.

7. Security

Your workspace needs your sign-in. Connections use secure, encrypted links and you can revoke them at any time.

8. Contact

Questions, access or deletion requests: hello@launchriot.online or +971 58 644 4285.
