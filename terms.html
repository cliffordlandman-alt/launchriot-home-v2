Terms of use

These are the plain-words terms for the LaunchRiot executive setup and workspace.

1. What the service is

A private workspace that brings your email, calendar, chat messages, tasks and follow-ups into one place, and prepares draft replies for you. LaunchRiot sets it up with you by hand.

2. Your permission and control

You decide what to connect and can disconnect at any time. The workspace never sends a message, or deletes or changes anything, on your behalf without your approval.

3. Chat messages

The chat connection works as a linked device on your own account. The chat app does not officially support this kind of connection, so there is a risk that it could limit or block your account. The connection is read-only, and replies are drafts that you send yourself. You choose whether to connect it.

4. Your responsibilities

Keep your sign-in details private, use the service lawfully, and only connect accounts you are allowed to connect. Check drafts before you approve them: they are aids, not advice.

5. Your data

Your data stays yours. How we handle it is explained in the Privacy notice. We do not sell it.

7. Availability

We work to keep the service running but cannot promise it will always be available or free of errors. Features may change as the product develops.

8. Limits of responsibility

The service is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, LaunchRiot is not liable for indirect losses, or for decisions made using drafts or suggestions. Nothing here limits rights you cannot lawfully give up.

9. Ending the service

You can stop at any time by disconnecting your accounts and asking us to delete your data. We may suspend access that breaks these terms or puts others at risk.
